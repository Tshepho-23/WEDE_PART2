Our story

From one rail of trainers to two stores

How a single street-side stall became Soweto's go-to for sneakers and streetwear.

Our history

Kasi Kicks started in 2019 as a single rail of resold sneakers at the Vilakazi Street market. What began as a side hustle turned into a full store once regulars started asking for sizes, apparel, and accessories to match — not just shoes.

Today, Kasi Kicks runs a flagship store in Soweto and a pop-up counter in Braamfontein, serving customers who want current styles at prices that make sense for the kasi.

Mission

To make quality sneakers and streetwear accessible to young people in Soweto and beyond, without the markup or the wait.

Vision

To become the most trusted independent sneaker and streetwear retailer in Johannesburg, known for fair prices and real community ties.

Target audience: teens and young adults (16–30) in Johannesburg who follow sneaker and streetwear culture and shop both online and in-store.
